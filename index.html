SiteCalc Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SiteCalc Pro ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

SiteCalc Pro is a construction materials calculator designed to help users estimate quantities of construction materials and related requirements for construction work.

1. Information We Collect

SiteCalc Pro does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, telephone number, postal address, or email address in order to use the App's core features.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

2. Calculator Inputs and Results

Information entered into the calculators, such as dimensions, quantities, material selections, mix ratios, waste percentages, reinforcement details, and other calculation inputs, is used to perform calculations and generate results.

The App's core calculation functions process these inputs locally on the user's device. We do not operate a SiteCalc Pro online account system or a server-based database for storing users' calculator inputs.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

SiteCalc Pro may store application preferences and settings locally on your device. These may include settings such as the selected theme, unit system, and other preferences required for the App to operate.

Locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to the SiteCalc Pro developer.

4. PDF Reports and Files

SiteCalc Pro can generate PDF reports containing calculation results. These reports are created and stored using the device's application storage facilities.

The user controls what happens to generated reports, including whether a report is shared, printed, or otherwise transferred from the device.

5. Sharing Reports

If you choose to share a PDF report, SiteCalc Pro uses the device's standard sharing functionality to allow you to select another application or service.

Once information is shared with another application or service, that application or service may process the information according to its own privacy policy and terms. SiteCalc Pro does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications or services selected by the user.

6. Analytics and Advertising

SiteCalc Pro does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or personalized advertising purposes.

The current version of SiteCalc Pro does not include a dedicated advertising or analytics service as part of its core application functionality.

7. Third-Party Services and Platform Features

SiteCalc Pro may use standard Android platform features and third-party software libraries required to provide functions such as local preferences, PDF generation, printing, file handling, font rendering, and sharing.

These components are used to provide application functionality. Where a user chooses to interact with another application or service through an Android platform feature, the handling of information by that third party is governed by the third party's own privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

SiteCalc Pro is a general-purpose construction calculation application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the collection of information by the App. Because the App's core calculations are performed locally on the user's device, users should protect their device and any files or reports stored on it.

10. Your Choices and Control

You may stop using SiteCalc Pro at any time and may uninstall the App using the normal Android device settings.

Depending on your Android device and settings, locally stored application data and preferences may be removed when the App is uninstalled or when its application data is cleared.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if SiteCalc Pro's features or information practices change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

12. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of SiteCalc Pro, please use the contact information provided on the SiteCalc Pro Google Play Store listing.
